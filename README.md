# NutriScale
NutriScale is a smart nutrition and health management web application designed to help users understand their nutritional condition, receive personalized food recommendations, and access healthy food products through an integrated marketplace.

The platform combines health assessment, AI-assisted nutrition insights, meal planning, marketplace features, shopping cart, checkout, payment flow, order history, and admin management into one complete application.

NutriScale supports multiple health profiles, including general users, children under five, pregnant women, and post-operation patients. By adapting recommendations to each user's condition, NutriScale helps make nutrition guidance more accessible, practical, and relevant for daily decision-making.

## Purpose

NutriScale was developed to support better nutrition awareness and healthier food choices through an accessible digital platform. The application helps users record essential health information, understand their nutritional status, and receive recommendations based on their profile and needs.

This goal aligns with Sustainable Development Goal 2: Zero Hunger, especially in improving nutrition and promoting access to safe, nutritious, and sufficient food.

NutriScale contributes by encouraging informed dietary decisions, supporting vulnerable health groups, and connecting users with healthier food options.

## Main Features

1. **Health Assessment**
   - A step-by-step assessment form that collects user health data, including basic information, anthropometric data, activity level, health goals, and special conditions.

2. **Special Condition Profiles**
   - Supports general users, children under five, pregnant women, and post-operation patients.

3. **Health Dashboard**
   - The dashboard displays important health and nutrition information, such as: nutritional status, calorie needs, AI insights, health tips, and recommended meal plans.

4. **AI Recommendations**
   - NutriScale provides personalized nutrition narratives and food recommendations based on the user's health profile and assessment results.

5. **Healthy Food Marketplace**
   - The marketplace allows users to browse healthy food products with features such as: search, category filters, calorie information, prices, and shopping cart functionality.
